NFIRS-5 · Basic Module · Draft Generator

Speak the call. Get the draft.

Tap the mic, speak ~30 seconds about what happened on the call, review the structured NFIRS-1 draft with codes pre-filled, paste it into your existing NFIRS-5 entry tool. Built by a fire-service volunteer + software engineer. No PHI. No accounts. No upsell. Free.

1. Describe the call

Voice or typed. Mention what you remember: address, time, what you found on arrival, what you did, who was hurt (if anyone), what caused it. The draft fills in the rest.

⌘/Ctrl + Enter to submit · max 4000 chars

2. Draft NFIRS-1 — review before filing

Hover any field to copy the value.

This is a DRAFT. The human filer is responsible for accuracy before NFIRS submission. The app does not retain narrative input or output. No PHI, no PII, no patient-care data should be entered.
