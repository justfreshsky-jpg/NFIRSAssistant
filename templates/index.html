NFIRS is retired

Calendar-year 2026 incident submission is exclusively in NERIS. USFA says NFIRS edits ended January 31, 2026, and NFIRS became unavailable in February 2026.

This tool does not create NFIRS reports or codes. It also does not map the NERIS schema, import data, or submit an incident.

1. Enter a de-identified call summary

Include only the operational facts needed for review: date or times, general property type, conditions found, actions taken, unit labels or counts, and outcomes.

Do not enter: exact addresses, names, phone numbers, email addresses, incident or case numbers, patient information, medical details, or sensitive operational information. Add required identifiers only inside your authorized NERIS or RMS system.

Voice recognition is provided by your browser and may use the browser vendor's speech service. Type instead if department policy does not allow that processing.

⌘/Ctrl + Enter to submit · maximum 4000 characters

2. Preparation aid — verify locally

Experimental AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Verify every item before entering it in NERIS or an RMS.
